# Employee Leave Policy

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays for their country of employment.

Up to 5 unused annual leave days can be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days must be used before 31 March, after which they expire.

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

Parental leave is 26 weeks fully paid for the primary caregiver and 6 weeks fully paid for the secondary caregiver. Leave requests should be submitted in the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance, except for sickness and emergencies.
