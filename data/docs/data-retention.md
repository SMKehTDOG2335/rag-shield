# Data Retention

Application logs are retained for 30 days on the Free and Pro plans and for 365 days on the Enterprise plan.

Database backups are taken every 6 hours and kept for 35 days. Point-in-time recovery is available for any moment within the last 7 days.

When an account is deleted, all customer data is permanently erased within 30 days, and backups containing that data expire naturally within the 35-day backup window.

Customers can export all of their data at any time in JSON or Parquet format from the Settings page. Exports larger than 50 GB are delivered as a signed download link that expires after 72 hours.
