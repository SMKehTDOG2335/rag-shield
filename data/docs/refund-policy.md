# Refund Policy

Stratus Labs offers a full refund on any annual subscription cancelled within 30 days of purchase. Monthly subscriptions are not refundable, but you can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the current billing cycle.

To request a refund, open a ticket from the Billing page of the dashboard and choose "Refund request". Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 7 to 10 business days.

Usage-based charges such as extra storage, GPU hours and bandwidth overages are never refundable, because the resources were already consumed.

If a service outage breaches our uptime SLA, customers receive service credits instead of cash refunds. Credits equal 10% of the monthly fee for each full hour of downtime, capped at 50% of that month's bill.
